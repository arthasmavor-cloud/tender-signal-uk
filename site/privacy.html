Privacy notice

Pre-launch notice: CivicBid is currently accepting enquiries only; self-service checkout is not yet available. Operator and business-address details will be published before paid subscriptions open. This notice is a practical draft, not legal advice.

Who we are

CivicBid is a UK public-procurement opportunity alert service. Until a dedicated CivicBid domain is connected, privacy enquiries can be sent to tendersignal.uk+privacy@gmail.com.

Information we use

We process business contact details, company and profile criteria, support communications and technical delivery records. We use these to respond to enquiries, provide requested opportunity alerts, prevent duplicate messages and operate the service.

Lawful basis and sharing

Where a subscription is live, we process service data to perform the contract with the subscriber. We may process limited operational data for legitimate interests such as service security and reliability. We use service providers only where needed to host the site, send email or process payments. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and rights

We keep account and delivery records only for as long as required for the service, legal obligations and dispute resolution. You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where applicable by emailing the address above. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Marketing and alerts

Service alerts are sent only to addresses supplied for a subscriber profile. Each message includes an unsubscribe route, and we suppress the address from future alerts when requested.
